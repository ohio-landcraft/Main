The first question

What happens to water after the dirt is moved?

Site work changes drainage. Pad elevations, cut-and-fill transitions, access routes and runoff should be considered before excavation begins, not after standing water appears.

Whole-property thinking

Look for the cause.

  • Finished floor and pad elevation
  • Positive drainage away from structures
  • Cut slopes and fill transitions
  • Equipment access during and after construction
  • Utility routes
  • Future septic, driveway and drainage needs
