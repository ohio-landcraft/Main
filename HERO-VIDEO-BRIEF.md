# Ohio Landcraft — Homepage Hero Video Brief

The homepage is now coded to use a **Vimeo-hosted muted, looping background video**. Paste the final Vimeo URL into the `data-vimeo-url` setting in `index.html`. See `VIMEO-SETUP.md` for the exact steps.

If no valid Vimeo URL is present, the site automatically falls back to `assets/images/home-hero.jpg`.

## Recommended Length
15–20 seconds, designed to loop cleanly.

## Recommended Format
- Upload the final video to Vimeo
- 1920 × 1080 minimum recommended
- 16:9 landscape
- No important audio is needed because the background version is muted
- Keep the important action away from the far-left headline area
- Slow, stable camera movement; no rapid cuts or aggressive speed ramps

## Suggested Storyboard

### 0–3 sec — The property
A calm drone move over an attractive Ohio property: driveway, rolling ground, woods, open space, or another setting that shows the land first.

**Message:** The property is the subject, not the equipment.

### 3–6 sec — Understand what the owner wants
Owner and Ohio Landcraft person walking the site, looking at the driveway/land, or reviewing an aerial/tablet together.

**Message:** Start with the outcome and involve the customer.

### 6–9 sec — Think it through
A brief glimpse of an aerial, site sketch, 3D property capture, or proposed 3D option on a screen.

**Message:** Planning is available when it helps make the decision easier.

### 9–13 sec — Use the right tools
A mini excavator, skid steer, grader or other appropriately sized equipment doing precise work. It should feel controlled and efficient rather than like a heavy-equipment hype reel.

Possible shots:
- pulling migrated gravel back into a driveway,
- shaping a ditch or crown,
- selective clearing,
- preparing a small building area.

**Message:** Equipment is a tool; use the tool the job actually needs.

### 13–17 sec — The result
Finished driveway, usable reclaimed space, clean drainage improvement, prepared site or another visibly improved part of the property.

**Message:** The customer is buying the outcome.

### 17–20 sec — Enjoy it
Vehicle smoothly using the driveway, owner walking the new space, family/property use, or a quiet finished-property shot that can blend back into the opening drone scene.

**Message:** Vision → Plan → Craft → Enjoy.

## Visual Style
The video should feel like a moving version of the Ohio Landcraft brand:
- natural Ohio landscape,
- clean and calm,
- thoughtful,
- property-focused,
- authentic,
- modern without feeling corporate.

Avoid:
- equipment-only montage,
- excessive mud/dust glamour shots,
- fast-cut action editing,
- giant truck/equipment hero shots,
- oversaturated HDR,
- dramatic logo stingers in the background loop.

## Important Hero-Text Consideration
The homepage text sits on the left. Keep that side visually simpler and darker enough to maintain white-text legibility.

## Optional Long-Form Version
The same footage can also be edited into a 45–90 second homepage/about video with narration. The background hero should remain the quiet 15–20 second loop.
