Common questions

Cabin-owner questions

Do I need to be at the property for an evaluation?

Not necessarily. We can begin with the address, your description, photos and available site information. If a field visit is appropriate, we can document what we see and review it with you remotely.

What if my property manager already handles maintenance?

That can work well. We can complement a property manager when the issue is primarily outside the cabin — driveway, drainage, access, brush, grading or a broader land-improvement decision.

Can you work with a contractor I already use?

Yes. You do not have to replace a contractor you trust. We can provide another perspective, plan around their work, or help when the project extends beyond their normal scope.
