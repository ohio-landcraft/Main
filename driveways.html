Driveways

A driveway that works better — and needs less attention.

Ohio Landcraft helps property owners repair, reshape and improve gravel driveways with the goal of better performance, sensible material use and less repeat maintenance.

Tell Us What You WantSee Capabilities
Start with the outcome

What do you want from the driveway?

Maybe you want fewer potholes, less washout, better access in bad weather, or simply a smoother drive that does not need constant stone and grading.

Sometimes the answer is simple.

If the existing shape is good and all you need is a grading and a couple loads of gravel, we will tell you. If a little more thought can reduce how much material you need or keep it in place longer, we will explain that too.

Capabilities

The work that may help get you there.

We use what the property and the desired result call for — not a fixed package.

  • Gravel driveway repair
  • Regrading and reshaping
  • Crown correction
  • New gravel installation
  • Rutting and pothole repair
  • Washout repair
  • Driveway widening
  • Parking and pull-off areas
  • Culvert installation or replacement
  • Ditch and edge drainage
  • Base stabilization
  • New access drives
What we think through

Enough planning to make the work make sense.

  • Where existing gravel has migrated and whether it can be reused
  • Where new aggregate truly adds value
  • How water gets off the driveway and where it goes next
  • Whether ditches, shoulders and culverts are helping or hurting
  • Which machine size completes the work efficiently without unnecessary disturbance
  • Whether current ground conditions are a good time to do the work
Make every dollar useful

Use the gravel you already paid for when it still has value.

More skilled grading and reshaping can sometimes reduce new aggregate, trucking and repeat repairs.

With material, hauling and fuel representing a meaningful share of a driveway project, a little more planning can produce significant savings over a two- or three-year horizon — without promising that every proper repair will be the cheapest upfront.

The right fit for the work

One Landcraft relationship. The right people and equipment for the job.

Rural property work often overlaps several skills. Through our trusted partner network, we can match the scope with the operator, equipment, hauling, materials or specialty experience it actually needs instead of forcing every project through one crew.

Landcraft stays your point of contact.

When a specialty partner is part of the field work, Ohio Landcraft helps define the scope, coordinate the project and keep the larger property goal in view. You should know who is coming, what they are doing and how that work fits the plan.

Knowledge transfer

Know how to keep it working.

We explain the shape, drainage and maintenance choices so you can recognize small issues early and handle simple upkeep yourself when appropriate.

  • Know where water is supposed to leave the driveway
  • Recognize berms or migrated stone before they become larger problems
  • Keep important culvert and ditch inlets open
  • Know when a simple rake or grading pass is enough — and when it is time to call
Need help deciding?

A simple project or a bigger idea — either is fine.

Start with what you want to accomplish. We can help you determine the practical next step.
