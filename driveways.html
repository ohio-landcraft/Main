The question we ask first

Is the driveway failing — or is water causing the driveway to fail?

A driveway can look like a stone problem while the real issue is concentrated runoff, inadequate crown, poor ditching, a failed culvert or a saturated base.

Whole-property thinking

Look for the cause.

  • Where uphill runoff enters the drive
  • Whether the crown is moving water to the edges
  • Whether ditches and outlets are actually functioning
  • Whether culverts are sized and positioned appropriately
  • Where the base is soft or pumping
  • Which areas need material versus reshaping
A practical process
