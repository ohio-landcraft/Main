Drainage

Get the water where you want it to go.

Drainage work should help you use and enjoy the property — a drier yard, a driveway that holds up better, or a building area that is ready for what comes next.

Tell Us What You WantSee Capabilities
Start with the outcome

What do you want to be dry, usable or protected?

Start with the outcome. We can then think through how water enters the area, how it moves, and where it can reasonably go.

Sometimes the answer is simple.

Sometimes a simple downspout extension or shallow swale is enough. Other times a culvert, grading change or subsurface drain makes sense. We do not start by assuming every water issue needs the same product.

Capabilities

The work that may help get you there.

We use what the property and the desired result call for — not a fixed package.

  • Downspout extensions and drainage
  • Surface swales
  • French drains where appropriate
  • Driveway drainage
  • Culverts
  • Ditch shaping
  • Yard drainage
  • Standing-water correction
  • Erosion repair
  • Grading for positive drainage
  • Outlet improvements
  • Runoff redirection
What we think through

Enough planning to make the work make sense.

  • The source of the water and when the issue occurs
  • The simplest safe path for water to leave the area
  • Whether surface grading can solve the issue before adding pipe
  • How the work affects nearby driveways, structures and future improvements
  • Equipment access and the amount of disturbance required
  • Timing work to avoid creating more lawn or soil damage than necessary
Make every dollar useful

Do not buy a complicated drainage system when a simpler path works.

We look for the level of intervention that gets the result you want without adding unnecessary materials, trenching or restoration.

The most economical solution is often the one that solves the actual use problem while creating the least extra disturbance.

The right fit for the work

One Landcraft relationship. The right people and equipment for the job.

Rural property work often overlaps several skills. Through our trusted partner network, we can match the scope with the operator, equipment, hauling, materials or specialty experience it actually needs instead of forcing every project through one crew.

Landcraft stays your point of contact.

When a specialty partner is part of the field work, Ohio Landcraft helps define the scope, coordinate the project and keep the larger property goal in view. You should know who is coming, what they are doing and how that work fits the plan.

Knowledge transfer

Understand where the water is supposed to go.

After the work, we explain the intended flow path and simple maintenance that can keep it performing.

  • Know which inlets, swales or outlets should stay clear
  • Understand what normal post-storm conditions should look like
  • Spot sediment, debris or erosion before they become larger issues
  • Handle straightforward maintenance yourself when appropriate
Need help deciding?

A simple project or a bigger idea — either is fine.

Start with what you want to accomplish. We can help you determine the practical next step.
