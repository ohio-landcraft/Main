Case studies

The problem. The thinking. The result.

This page is intentionally built around case studies rather than a generic equipment gallery. Replace these examples with real Ohio Landcraft projects as you complete them.

Driveway + drainage

Recurring washout on a sloped gravel drive

Problem: Gravel repeatedly washed out after heavy rain.

What we found: Uphill runoff entered the drive before the crown could shed it.

Approach: Intercept runoff, improve the outlet, reshape the drive and add material only where needed.

Result: A repair aimed at stopping the cycle instead of simply replacing lost stone.

Clearing + access

Overgrown acreage with limited usable access

Problem: Brush and volunteer growth reduced visibility and made sections difficult to reach.

What we found: Not every area needed the same level of clearing.

Approach: Prioritize access, preserve useful screening and create maintainable edges.

Result: More usable land without unnecessarily stripping the property.

Site preparation

Future garage site on rolling terrain

Problem: Create a practical pad and access route on sloping ground.

What we found: Pad work would affect both runoff and future utility routes.

Approach: Coordinate elevation, drainage, access and utility trenching before final grading.

Result: A site prepared for the next phase instead of isolated earthwork.
