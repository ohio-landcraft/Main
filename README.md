## What Changed

The site now starts with what the customer wants to accomplish rather than “diagnosing a problem.” It emphasizes:

- simple solutions when simple solutions are appropriate,
- material reuse,
- right-sized equipment,
- timing work to avoid unnecessary disturbance/cost,
- phased planning,
- total cost over time rather than cheapest upfront price,
- customer participation and knowledge transfer,
- optional drone/ground-photo 3D property planning,
- SEO-friendly service language without keyword stuffing.
